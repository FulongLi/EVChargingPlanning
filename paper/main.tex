\begin{abstract}
This paper proposes a grid-aware demand clustering framework for electric vehicle charging station siting and sizing. The method partitions urban charging demand into interpretable service zones using demand, accessibility, and grid-risk proxy features, and then performs location-capacity optimisation within each zone. A London case study is designed to evaluate the proposed method against geographical clustering, demand-weighted clustering, and baseline siting strategies.
\end{abstract}
